Services

Four kinds of work, with exactly what is included in each. If your problem is not on this page, ask anyway — I will tell you honestly whether it is something I can build well.

Billing, stock & khata software

For shops, medical stores, distributors and small traders still running on a register and a calculator.

  • GST-ready bills and printed receipts
  • Stock that reduces itself with every sale
  • Low-stock and expiry alerts
  • Customer khata: who owes what, since when
  • Day, month and item-wise reports
  • Runs on the shop computer and on your phone

Typical build: 2–4 weeks after the demo is approved.

WhatsApp automation

For clinics, salons, coaching centres and anyone whose phone rings with the same questions all day.

  • Instant replies to timing, fees, address, availability
  • Appointment requests captured into a sheet your staff can read
  • Reminders the day before, so fewer people forget
  • Handover to a human whenever the question is real
  • Works after hours, on Sundays, during the rush

Typical build: 1–2 weeks. Uses your own WhatsApp Business number.

Websites & online tools

For businesses that need to be findable, and for anyone with an idea for a tool people would use.

  • Fast, mobile-first pages — no page-builder bloat
  • Enquiry forms that reach your WhatsApp or email
  • Catalogues, calculators, booking pages
  • Search-engine basics done properly at build time
  • Hosting set up in your own account, in your name

Typical build: 1–2 weeks.

Custom automation

For the job somebody in your office does by hand every single day, the same way, forever.

  • Reports pulled, cleaned and mailed on a schedule
  • Data moved between Excel, software and websites
  • Bulk file work: renaming, merging, converting, uploading
  • Alerts when a number crosses a limit you set
  • Runs unattended and tells you if it ever fails

Typical build: a few days to two weeks.
