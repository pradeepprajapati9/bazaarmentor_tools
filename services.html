Services

Four kinds of work, with exactly what is included in each. If your problem is not on this page, ask anyway — you will get an honest answer about whether we can build it well.

Billing, stock & khata

Shops · medical stores · distributors

For businesses still running on a register, a calculator and a very good memory.

  • GST-ready bills and printed receipts
  • Stock that reduces itself with every sale
  • Low-stock and expiry alerts before you lose money
  • Customer khata: who owes what, since when
  • Day, month and item-wise reports
  • Runs on the shop computer and on your phone
Typical build: 2–4 weeks

WhatsApp automation

Clinics · salons · coaching centres

For anyone whose phone rings all day with the same five questions.

  • Instant replies on timing, fees, address, availability
  • Appointment requests captured into a sheet staff can read
  • Reminders the day before, so fewer people forget
  • Handover to a human the moment the question is real
  • Works after hours, on Sundays and through the rush
Typical build: 1–2 weeks

Websites & online tools

Local businesses · product ideas

For businesses that need to be findable, and for anyone with an idea for a tool people would use.

  • Fast, mobile-first pages — no page-builder bloat
  • Enquiry forms that land on your WhatsApp or email
  • Catalogues, calculators and booking pages
  • Search-engine basics done properly at build time
  • Hosting set up in your own account, in your name
Typical build: 1–2 weeks

Custom automation

Offices · back-office teams

For the job somebody does by hand every single day, the same way, forever.

  • Reports pulled, cleaned and mailed on a schedule
  • Data moved between Excel, software and websites
  • Bulk file work: renaming, merging, converting, uploading
  • Alerts when a number crosses a limit you set
  • Runs unattended, and tells you if it ever fails
Typical build: days to 2 weeks
