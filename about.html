About

A studio, not an agency

The developer who writes your software is the person who answers your call. No account manager in between, no team that changes every three months.

BazaarMentor builds software for businesses that are still running on registers, WhatsApp forwards and one very patient person who remembers everything.

The people behind it write hospital ERP software by day — the unglamorous kind that has to work on a Monday morning with a queue at the counter. That is a useful school. Hospital software is not about clever features; it is about a receptionist with thirty seconds per patient and no patience for a system that hangs. A shop counter is exactly the same. So the software here is built for the person actually using it, not for a demo video.

On using AI

We use AI tools to build faster, and we would rather say that plainly than pretend otherwise. It is the reason a working demo takes a week instead of two months.

What it does not mean is shipping code nobody has read or tested. Security, correctness and keeping your data safe stay a human responsibility. You are hiring a developer who is accountable, not a prompt.

What we will not do

Overpromise

If something is beyond what we can finish well, you hear that in the first conversation, not after the invoice.

Hold your data

Your records are exported and handed over whenever you ask. No lock-in, no hostage files.

Disappear

If it breaks after delivery, it is ours to fix. That is what the monthly fee is actually for.
