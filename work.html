Featherbyte Cookie Banner

Live

A cookie-consent app for Shopify stores. Existing consent apps slow stores down badly and break checkout; this one is deliberately light and handles Google Consent Mode correctly.

Why it counts: Shopify reviewed it themselves and approved it. It is listed publicly in their App Store, where anyone can install it.

TariffWise

Live

Works out US import duty, customs fees, total landed cost and a sensible selling price for people shipping goods into America.

Why it counts: built the month the $800 duty-free exemption ended, when no free tool for it existed. It is public, free and running today.

ResumeFit

Built

Scores a resume against a job description the way company screening software does — contact details, sections, keywords, impact, writing and formatting — then rebuilds it in a layout that parses cleanly.

Why it counts: eight templates, Word and PDF output, and the score stays the same after reformatting — proof the formatting does not break the parsing.

Front-desk WhatsApp bot

Working demo

Answers the timing, fees and location questions a clinic or salon front desk repeats fifty times a day, and drops appointment requests into a sheet the staff can read.

Why it counts: built to India's data-protection rules from the start — consent taken, only the details needed are stored.

Options signal system

Runs daily

Pulls market data on a schedule, applies a fixed set of rules and writes out the day's shortlist. Nobody presses a button.

Why it counts: it has run unattended every trading day, including through failures that it reports instead of hiding. This is the same discipline a business automation needs.
